Rollout options

One platform. Four ways to deploy.

Lumasite fits the workflow you already run. Choose the capture path that matches how your claims come in.

Policyholder at FNOL

Capture happens during the first-notice-of-loss call. The policyholder receives a link, walks through the damaged rooms, and takes the photos. A desk adjuster receives both the claim and the draft estimate.

Mitigation technician

Before or after mitigation work, the technician photographs the damaged rooms, noting pre-existing conditions and the full extent of the loss. A desk adjuster receives the documentation and a full draft estimate.

Field adjuster

Documentation takes minutes onsite instead of hours. By the time the adjuster is back at the desk, a full draft estimate is waiting for review.

Desk adjuster

The adjuster sends the policyholder a link, then receives a draft estimate without leaving their desk.

Anyone can capture a claim without compromising estimate quality — which means you can reserve your most experienced adjusters for QA and complex claims.
